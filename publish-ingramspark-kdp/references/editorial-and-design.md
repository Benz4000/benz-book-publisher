# Editorial and design acceptance

## Text and interiors

Read each manuscript individually in full, recovering truncated reads and recording version/hash and exact coverage. Check missing endings, character logic, chronology, repeated material, chapter joins, contents, and edition consistency. Read the complete integrated revision again after substantive edits. Preserve listed authors; do not invent biographies. Make substantial new fiction only within the user's authorization and document it. Validate translations against the complete source, including native titles and accents; disclose any absence of professional language review.

For recipes, check quantities, yields, times, method, doneness, storage, and internal consistency. Do not call generated recipes kitchen-tested. Verify consequential factual claims through current authoritative sources as needed; retain educational framing and avoid unsupported assurances.

Use consistent styles rather than ad hoc paragraph overrides. Ordinary prose and back summaries are justified with natural last lines, automatic hyphenation off. Inspect **rendered** multiline paragraph endings throughout the final output: no isolated final word; prefer at least three words where wording permits. Lightly reword without changing meaning or padding content, then re-render. Do not force a preceding line to stretch or use nonbreaking strings/manual breaks to fake compliance. Recheck pagination and obtain a replacement template if final page count changes.

Picture books use centered text with intentional phrase-based line breaks. Balance line lengths and keep a final phrase together rather than stranding its last word. Judge each spread with its illustration: preserve faces and focal areas, readable contrast, safe margins, and age-appropriate size. Intentional one-word dialogue, headings, poetry, and sound effects are not ordinary-prose orphan defects.

Check margins, selectable text where appropriate, embedded fonts, chapter openings, page numbers, ordered-list continuation, TOC, front/end matter, worksheets, and intended blank pages. EPUB should preserve reading order, navigation, language and accessible structure without unsupported accessibility claims. Run EPUBCheck and inspect the converted ebook/Kindle preview. Never assume print pagination matches reflowable ebook pagination.

## Full-image cover composition

Start from story/genre/content and the approved brief. Use original or licensed art with prompts/provenance recorded. Fill the physical front panel plus required bleed/turn-in; avoid blank framing caused by placing a padded front PDF inside the template's safe rectangle. Safe text margins constrain lettering, not the image background. Preserve composition without stretching people, lettering, or artwork. Extend backgrounds thoughtfully when aspect ratios differ.

Design large, readable title typography into the artwork's composition; place subtitle/author with deliberate alignment, scale, contrast, and negative space. Avoid slapped-on text, arbitrary horizontal bands, small boxed illustrations, and heavy outlines. Exact title, author, native-language spelling, and intact long words take priority over unreliable generated lettering. Separate typesetting is appropriate when it produces precise integrated lettering. Preserve important characters/faces when cropping.

For print, use the actual official template's front/back/spine/bleed geometry and preserve its original vector barcode and white quiet area where supplied. Decode the final barcode and match the expected ISBN. KDP barcode handling follows its current template and setup. No spine text when the book/template does not permit it. Never use a whole template sheet or print wrap as ebook art.

Standalone ebook art is front-only RGB at the current destination's required aspect ratio/dimensions. Choose final dimensions from current official destination requirements and the approved design brief. Record native source dimensions and effective print resolution. Upsampling/sharpening does not create native detail; do not conceal a resolution limitation.

Back summaries are justified with natural flush-left final paragraph lines, generous safe margins, and clear barcode/footer space. An optional starting layout for a 6x9 back is Georgia 14pt, 19.5pt leading, about 12pt paragraph gaps, and about 0.625in side margins. Confirm it suits the book and official template. A cream back does not imply a framed cream front. Do not silently shrink copy to fit: edit new copy accurately or adjust the approved layout. Preserve existing wording when it fits. No em dashes in new descriptions/back marketing copy. Keep hooks distinct and remove old summary text completely to prevent residual letters/hidden duplicates.

## Final checks and revisions

Inspect every cover front, back, full wrap, and interior, with page/spread coverage appropriate to the actual claimed review. Thumbnail sheets are triage, not substitutes for readable-size review. Investigate apparent thumbnail clipping against full-size source/proof. Inspect small subtitles, native characters, contrast, and any outlined/vector lettering especially closely. Isolate text rendering modes in drawing code (for example saveState/restoreState around ReportLab outlined titles) and render after every correction.

Check actual template dimensions, bleed/safety, font embedding, color treatment, ink coverage, barcode, resolution, and clipping. Use current destination requirements for color treatment and total ink coverage. Structural checks do not establish independent PDF/X certification, physical print/color fidelity, or kitchen testing.

Targeted corrections preserve everything outside scope. Back-summary-only means print cover only. Cover-only EPUB revisions preserve body, navigation, styles, and unrelated metadata: compare ZIP entries and bytes; if an OPF timestamp must change, normalize it and verify all other OPF semantics unchanged. Run EPUBCheck. Record corrected file/hash and visual evidence before upload.

For already-uploaded defects, maintain a correction log with title/ISBN/profile/format, confirmed defect and evidence, checked platform status/date, corrected path/hash and checks, next step, and eventual submission evidence. Keep resolved history. Never rerun stale broad builders or summary scripts that can regress ledgers or alter submitted files.
