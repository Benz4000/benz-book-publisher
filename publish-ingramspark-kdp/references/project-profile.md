# Project decisions

Create a private per-project record before platform-specific production. Resolve existing choices first; ask only for missing decisions that affect the release. This template supplies no standing publishing permission.

- Destination: IngramSpark, KDP, or separately packaged releases for both.
- Title, edition, language, requested formats, listed contributors.
- Copyright owner, year, publisher credit, imprint, identifier ownership and applicable ISBN terms.
- Manuscript provenance, translation scope, rights evidence, accurate AI disclosures.
- Trim, binding, paper, finish, bleed, final page count and official template.
- Body styles, picture-book treatment, cover brief, back-cover typography.
- Prices, markets, discount/return settings where applicable, enrollment choice, compensation.
- Publication date and any deadline.
- Authorized endpoint and exclusions: preparation, submission, proof approval, distribution, fees.
- Current source/release hashes, platform identifiers, dated evidence, pending corrections.

Keep actual account identifiers, private email, credentials, manuscripts, and customer data out of public repositories. Distinguish approved decisions from proposed defaults. Never inherit another project's prices, identities, ISBN acceptance, AI answers, or permissions.
