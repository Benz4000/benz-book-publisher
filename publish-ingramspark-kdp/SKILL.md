# Publish for IngramSpark or KDP

Produce a complete, checked release for the requested destination: **IngramSpark or KDP**. Resolve the platform, edition, formats, collection, and authorized endpoint from the request and records. When both platforms are requested, maintain separate release packages and status records. If the destination is genuinely unspecified, complete shared editorial work while clarifying it before platform-specific production.

## Shared acceptance standard

- Read source and corrected manuscripts completely; preserve originals and document actual reading coverage. Automated scans, successful conversion, and platform validation do not establish editorial completion.
- Justify ordinary body prose and back-cover summaries, with natural flush-left final paragraph lines. Disable automatic hyphenation. Eliminate isolated one-word paragraph endings through restrained editing that preserves meaning. Aim for at least three words on the last line of multiline prose paragraphs where natural. Do not force spacing, nonbreaking spaces, hard breaks, overhang tricks, filler, or distorted letter widths to obtain this result.
- Center picture-book text. Break lines at natural phrases with balanced lengths; move a phrase together instead of leaving one word dangling. Preserve intentional short lines, dialogue, poetry, labels, and headings where their form is meaningful. Do not justify picture-book text into stretched lines.
- Fill the actual front cover with immersive, book-specific artwork. Compose the exact title, subtitle, and listed author as an intentional part of the design, with readable hierarchy, contrast, and breathing room. Do not center a miniature finished cover on a large blank/cream page, add arbitrary bars or boxes, or use thick outlines as a substitute for design. Typography may be separately typeset for precision; it must visually belong to the artwork.
- Review each final interior, front, back, and wrap at readable size and each front at thumbnail size. Check rendered line endings and lettering; extraction and contact sheets alone cannot prove readability.
- Finalize interior pagination, then obtain the destination's official template for the exact format, trim, binding, paper, page count, and identifier as applicable **before building the final cover**. Paperback and hardcover need their own templates; IngramSpark and KDP files are not interchangeable.

Read [editorial-and-design.md](references/editorial-and-design.md) for production acceptance criteria. Read only the relevant platform section in [platform-workflows.md](references/platform-workflows.md). Record the current project choices using [project-profile.md](references/project-profile.md); private catalog history is not included in this package.

## Work to the requested endpoint

1. **Reconcile before changing anything.** Read current source, edition plan, manifest, correction log, ISBN/profile register, and newest checkpoints. When account work is requested, reconcile visible live records by title + edition + format + ISBN/profile. Historical counts and sent messages are not completed work. Resume existing drafts without duplicate profiles or identifiers.
2. **Edit and format.** Complete missing content within authorization, check continuity and claims, read the revised whole, then paginate. Retain full narrative scope; never silently turn a novel into an abridgment. Record substantial new authorship and truthful actual length.
3. **Build the destination package.** Preserve source and prior versions. Include final interiors, official-template print wraps, front-only ebook art, EPUB where requested, metadata, exact hashes, checks, and a release ledger. Verify current official technical specifications when producing a live release rather than encoding changeable platform limits here.
4. **Validate every edition.** Complete editorial, typography, visual, technical, and format checks separately. Confirm the reviewed bytes match the final upload files. Rebuild only affected outputs and invalidate downstream reviews when their files change.
5. **Upload and complete authorized submission.** Use supported authenticated UI or an available sufficient connector, with fresh observed state and exact edition files. Verify receipt and both content and metadata validation. Carry authorized submission through the actual completion action and verify the result. Continue the authorized batch without repeated questions about settled choices.
6. **Record and close.** Save dated evidence, identifiers, hashes, actual settings, and remaining blockers. Close only completed book tabs after verified submission and saved evidence. Reconcile account records before repeating an uncertain mutation, especially if the user is also working.

## Authorization and evidence

Preserve established authorization within its actual collection and action scope. This skill itself grants no permission for external actions. Do not add redundant confirmation gates; comply with any controlling tool's actual action-time requirements and batch concrete ready titles when confirmation is required. Stop if a fee appears unless that charge was separately authorized. Proof approval, distribution activation, orders, and payment are separate actions with separate scope. Do not approve an incorrect/stale proof to unlock a revision.

Use existing login or an authorized normal saved-login flow; ask for user involvement only when credentials or verification require it. Follow stricter collection-specific login preferences. Never embed credentials in this skill or records.

Keep distinct per-format stages: editorial checked, locally ready, uploaded, validated, submitted, processing, current proof ready, proof reviewed, proof approved, distribution enabled, retail live, blocked. Record observed labels alongside these internal stages. In Processing does not establish the latest revision's successful submission. A dated submitted account record may confirm receipt when the confirmation page fails; save the actual evidence and its limits. Do not claim ebook verification from a print proof, or retailer availability from distribution enablement.

Ledger minimum: collection/title/edition/language/format; author/copyright owner/imprint; ISBN and platform IDs; trim/paper/binding/pages; source and submitted paths/SHA256; editorial and visual review scope; persisted commercial settings; dated upload/validation/submission/proof/distribution evidence; unresolved corrections and next action. Preserve history rather than replacing it with stale summaries.

For shared-account work, honor an existing browser lease and disjoint ownership. Do not steal locks or overwrite another worker's records. Do not create chats, agents, or monitors solely because past batches used them. If a requested monitor is created, keep it quiet while unchanged and respect pause/stop; monitoring never implies publishing authority.
